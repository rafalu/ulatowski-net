DAY 1

Monday — Bari

Vecchia + Murat · arrival ~10:00 · no train needed

You land around 10:00. No trains today. Drop the bags, settle in, and get to know the city on foot. Bari Vecchia is one of Italy’s most atmospheric medieval quarters — pasta-making nonnas in the street, a Norman castle, and Romanesque basilicas.

🥐
Breakfast

Caffè Borghese (Via Putignani, Murat) — cornetto + espresso €2–3. ⚠️ verify

🏃
Run — Lungomare loop

6 km · Piazza Diaz → Pane e Pomodoro beach → back. Flat asphalt, waterfront promenade, very safe from 06:30 onward.

Alternative run — Parco 2 Giugno

3–5 km paved loop inside Bari’s largest park (Murat). Shaded by Mediterranean pines, flat, water fountains. Great when the seafront is too windy. ~15 min walk from base.

☀️
Morning
  • Piazza Mercantile → Piazza del Ferrarese → Strada Arco Basso (orecchiette nonnas, mornings only)
  • Basilica di San Nicola — Saint Nicholas’ relics in the crypt, Norman architecture
  • Cattedrale di San Sabino — 5 min walk away
🍝
Lunch

Le Travi – La Braceria (Largo Chiurlia) — orecchiette cime di rapa, grilled meats. €€. Reservation recommended.
Or: Panificio Fiore (Strada Palazzo di Città 38) — takeaway focaccia barese, €2.50 per slice.

🌅
Afternoon
  • Castello Svevo — walls + views (interior €6, ~45 min)
  • Muraglia — old wall promenade, best at sunset
🍷
Aperitivo

Enoteca Vinarius (Via Marchese di Montrone, Murat) — Primitivo di Manduria or Susumaniello, ~€5–7. ⚠️ verify

🏗️
Dinner

Vini e Cucina (Strada Vallisa) — historic trattoria, fava e cicoria + orecchiette. €. No reservations, arrive at 19:30.
Backup: La Uascezze — orecchiette + horse meat (regional). ⚠️ verify Monday opening
Alternative: Terranima (Via Putignani, Murat) — cozy trattoria, excellent orecchiette, less touristy. €€.

🌧️
Rain backup

Teatro Petruzzelli tour + Pinacoteca Provinciale

🥾
Walking intensity

Medium (~10–12 km including the run). Bring a light evening layer, comfortable shoes, sunglasses, and a water bottle.

DAY 2

Tuesday — Polignano a Mare

🚆 Trenitalia 25 min · €3.20 · cliffs, old town, Lama Monachile

The iconic cliffs of southern Italy. Go early before the day-trip buses arrive. A whitewashed old town hanging above the Adriatic, and the birthplace of Domenico Modugno (“Volare”).

🥐
Breakfast

Before the train: Martinucci Laboratory (Murat, Bari). Or in Polignano: Super Mago del Gelo — the famous caffè speciale (espresso + amaretto + lemon zest + cream). ⚠️ verify

🏃
Run — South cliff route

5 km out-and-back · Piazza Aldo Moro → Cala Paguro / Cala San Giovanni. Asphalt + dirt. Stunning sea views. Watch the cliff edges — some stretches have no barriers.

Alternative run — North to San Vito

5 km out-and-back from the station north along SP87 toward San Vito / Cala Incina. Wider road, fewer cliff edges, mix of farmland and sea glimpses. Flatter and safer.

🚆
Getting there
  • Walk to Bari Centrale (~10 min)
  • Trenitalia R/RV → Polignano a Mare (~25–30 min, €3.20)
  • Tickets: Trenitalia app or station machines · e-tickets do not need validation
  • Return: trains until ~23:00, comfortable last train around ~22:00
☀️
Morning
  • From the station, walk 10 min to Arco Marchesale
  • Lama Monachile — photo from the Roman bridge above the cove
  • Old town: Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Modugno statue, Terrazza Santo Stefano
🍝
Lunch

Pescaria (Piazza Aldo Moro) — gourmet seafood panini (tuna tartare, octopus). €€. Queue peaks 13:00–14:00, so go at 12:00 or 14:30.
Or: Mint Cucina Fresca — modern seafood. €€. Reservation recommended.
Hidden gem: Osteria di Chichibio (old town) — local seafood, quieter, off the tourist circuit. €€.

🌅
Afternoon
  • Grotta Palazzese — look from above, do not eat there (€€€€, mixed reviews)
  • Sea cave boat tour (45–60 min, €15–20 pp from the harbor). Cash.
  • Or simply sit by Lama Monachile
🏖️
Beach

Lama Monachile — pebbly, crowded, but iconic. May water: 18–20°C. A brave dip = yes. A proper beach day = no.

🍷
Aperitivo

Il Bastione — cliff terrace, spritz or Verdeca. ⚠️ verify

🍽️
Dinner

Back in Bari. Al Pescatore (Piazza Federico II) — seafood, terrace. €€–€€€. Reservation recommended.
Cheap backup: Panzerotteria Di Cosimo — panzerotti for €2 each.

🎒
Bring

Swimsuit + microfibre towel, hat, cash, and your phone for the bridge photo.

DAY 3

Wednesday — Monopoli

🚆 Trenitalia 35–45 min · €3.80 · harbor, castle, beach within the walls

The older, calmer cousin of Polignano. A real fishing harbor with painted gozzi boats, an Aragonese castle by the sea, fewer Instagram crowds, and better food for the money. The town locals would quietly recommend instead.

🥐
Breakfast

Lula Caffè (Piazza XX Settembre, Monopoli) on arrival. ⚠️ verify

🏃
Run — Lungomare Santa Maria

4–5 km · flat waterfront route → Cala Porta Vecchia → Castello. Quiet and safe in the morning. Reward yourself with a cornetto in the old town afterward.

Alternative run — South to Capitolo

5–6 km out-and-back along the coast road (SP90) south from Monopoli. Wider road, flat, olive groves on one side and rocky coves on the other. More open and rural.

🚆
Getting there
  • Bari Centrale → Monopoli, Trenitalia regional, ~35–45 min, €3.80
  • From the station: 15 min walk to the old town
  • Return: until ~22:30
☀️
Morning
  • Castello Carlo V + harbor walls
  • Cattedrale Maria SS. della Madia — baroque, gold-lit interior
  • Porto Vecchio — painted fishing boats
🍝
Lunch

Osteria Perricci (Via Orazio Comes) — a Monopoli institution since the 1920s, seafood antipasti, orecchiette frutti di mare. €€. Reservation recommended. ⚠️ verify
Raw-fish option: Il Guazzetto (old town, near Piazza Palmieri) — crudo di mare and grilled catch of the day. €€.

🏖️
Beach

Cala Porta Vecchia — a tiny beach tucked into the city walls. Very postcard-worthy. In May, swimming is optimistic.

🚲
Alternative afternoon — e-bike

Rent an e-bike from Monopoli’s city bike sharing (45 stations, 200 e-bikes, official service) and ride south toward Capitolo beaches. Ask at the info point: Piazza Garibaldi 24, daily 10:00–19:00. ⚠️ verify

🍷
Aperitivo

Mezza Pinta (Via Vasco de Gama) — Negroamaro rosato + taralli. ⚠️ verify

🍽️
Dinner — ★ splurge night

Back to Bari for La Bul (Via Villari, Murat) — modern Puglia tasting menu, €€€. Book 1–2 weeks ahead. ⚠️ verify

🌧️
Rain backup

Castello Carlo V interior + lunch at Perricci + an afternoon at Bari’s Pinacoteca

DAY 4

Thursday — Trani

🚆 Trenitalia 35–45 min · ~€4 · cathedral by the sea, Giudecca, Moscato di Trani

The underrated star. An 11th-century Romanesque cathedral built right by the sea — one of the most beautiful churches in Italy, and much less crowded than Polignano. Fishing harbor, Jewish quarter, and a different coastline north of Bari. The change of direction keeps the trip fresh.

🥐
Breakfast

Quick stop near the base (for example Caffè Borghese) to catch the earliest easy train.

🏃
Run — Trani harbor loop

4 km loop · harbor → Villa Comunale → cathedral promenade. Very scenic and safe in the morning. Or do the Bari Lungomare before the train.

Alternative run — South to Bisceglie

5 km out-and-back along the Lungomare south past Colonna di Marmo. Flat coastal road, very quiet in the early morning, with views back toward Trani’s cathedral silhouette.

🚆
Getting there
  • Bari Centrale → Trani, Trenitalia R/RV, ~35–45 min, ~€4
  • From the station: ~12 min to the harbor via Via Cavour, flat walk
  • Return: until ~22:00
☀️
Morning
  • Cattedrale di San Nicola Pellegrino — cathedral by the sea. Three levels: crypt, lower church, upper church.
  • Castello Svevo di Trani — castle with a sea moat (€5)
🍝
Lunch

Corteinfiore (Via Ognissanti) — refined seafood, courtyard seating. €€–€€€. Reservation recommended.
Mid-range: Osteria Frangipane — burrata, raw fish, local wines. €€. ⚠️ verify
Harbor-front: La Darsena (Via Statuti Marittimi) — local seafood, more casual, right on the water. €€.

🌅
Afternoon
  • Giudecca — Jewish quarter, including the Scolanova synagogue, one of the oldest in Europe
  • Harbor stroll + gelato
🍷
Aperitivo — Moscato di Trani

Le Lampare al Fortino — terrace at the harbor fortification, sunset + Moscato di Trani DOC. Drink it here, not in Bari — this is its true home. ⚠️ verify

🍽️
Dinner

Stay in Trani: Quintessenza (Via Nigrò) — refined regional cuisine, sometimes Michelin-mentioned. €€€. Book 1+ week ahead. ⚠️ verify
Cheaper: Osteria Frangipane or head back to Bari.

📸
Bring

A respectful layer for the cathedral, cash for smaller shops, and a camera — the cathedral at golden hour is the shot of the trip.

DAY 4 · ALT

Thursday — Matera

🚆 FAL ~1h45–2h · Sassi, caves, sunset, wine · romantic stretch day

⚠️ Alternative to Trani. Use only if you accept longer transit (~1h45–2h each way via FAL) and higher walking intensity. Trani is the safer default. Check FAL timetable the day before — current disruption on Matera Sud–Altamura with bus replacement (valid from Dec 2025 "until further notice"). No FAL service on Sundays/holidays.

The most dramatic day of the trip. Matera’s Sassi — UNESCO cave houses, churches, monasteries built into natural caves — are cinematic, ancient, and genuinely romantic at sunset. It breaks the 1-hour rule, but if you want one unforgettable romantic day, this is it.

🥐
Breakfast

Quick coffee + cornetto near the base or Bari Centrale. Don’t waste time — the day is about getting to Matera early.

🏃
Run — Bari Lungomare shakeout

3–4 km at 06:30 if energy allows. Flat, easy. Do not run in Matera Sassi — steep stone stairs, slippery surfaces, tourists.

🚆
Getting there — FAL (not Trenitalia)
  • Walk to Bari Centrale / FAL side (~10 min, separate area)
  • FAL Bari Centrale → Matera Centrale, ~1h45–2h (bus replacement segment active)
  • Target: early departure 07:45–08:00
  • Return: 18:15–19:10 from Matera if dinner in Bari, or later if staying
  • ⚠️ Check FAL timetable the day before. If disrupted → keep Trani.
☀️
Morning
  • Arrive Matera Centrale → walk to Piazza Vittorio Veneto
  • First viewpoint: Belvedere Luigi Guerricchio (“Three Arches”)
  • Slow descent into Sasso Barisano
  • Casa Grotta di via Fiorentini — furnished cave-house museum (~€5)
  • Keep the first 2 hours loose. Matera rewards wandering, not speedrunning.
🍝
Lunch — Lucanian food

Small trattoria in Sasso Barisano / Caveoso. Try: pane di Matera (IGP bread), peperoni cruschi (fried dried peppers), cavatelli (local pasta), lamb, local cheese.
Romantic option: Baccanti Ristorante — cave setting in the Sassi. €€€. Reservation needed.

🌅
Afternoon
  • Walk toward Sasso Caveoso
  • Visit Madonna de Idris / San Giovanni in Monterrone OR Santa Lucia alle Malve — pick one, not all
  • Piazzetta Pascoli — one of the best viewpoints within the city
  • Do not overpack the schedule. Matera punishes checklist tourists.
🍷
Aperitivo — cave wine bar

Enoteca dai Tosi — cave wine bar in the Sassi. The romantic wine stop of the trip. Local Basilicata wine, especially Aglianico del Vulture. ⚠️ verify

🍽️
Dinner — choose your path

Stay in Matera: Baccanti (cave restaurant, book ahead) or Dimora Ulmo (refined, terrace if open). Return later.
Zero-risk: Return to Bari ~18:15–19:10, eat simple in Bari Vecchia. Do not book a tasting-menu dinner in Bari after Matera — that’s overkill.

🥾
Walking intensity — HIGH

Steep, uneven, full of stairs and stone. Beautiful but not soft. Wear grippy shoes, bring water + power bank.

🌧️
Rain / transport backup

Matera works in rain (caves, rock churches, wine bar, long lunch). But if transport is disrupted → keep Trani.

DAY 5

Friday — Bari → ✈️

Last coffee, last focaccia, FM2 to the airport

Flight around midday. No adventures today. One last espresso, one last focaccia, one last look at the sea, then the airport train.

🏃
Morning run

3 km shakeout on the Lungomare at 06:30. Back, shower, pack.

Alternative run — Parco 2 Giugno

3 km shaded loop — quieter than the seafront, a gentle farewell-to-Bari option if you’ve done the Lungomare enough.

🥐
Breakfast

Panificio Fiore — focaccia + coffee. Grab an extra slice for the road.

✈️
Airport transfer
  • Leave the base at 08:30–08:45
  • Walk → Bari Centrale (10 min)
  • Ferrotramviaria FM2 → Aeroporto di Bari (~15–17 min, €5–5.30 "Pay and Go", every 30 min). Station inside BRI, connected via 300 m tunnel to arrivals.
  • Tickets: separate counter inside Centrale (not Trenitalia) or the app
  • Paper tickets → validate in the yellow/green machine
  • Backup: Tempesta bus or Bolt/taxi €25–30

🍷 Puglia food & wine

🍝 Must-try dishes

•
Focaccia barese — tomato + olives, fluffy, oily, and perfect for breakfast. Benchmark: Panificio Fiore.
•
Orecchiette con cime di rapa — broccoli rabe + anchovy + garlic
•
Panzerotti — fried dough pockets with mozzarella + tomato. Di Cosimo / Mastro Ciccio.
•
Tiella di riso, patate e cozze — Bari’s rice, potato, and mussel bake
•
Burrata di Andria DOP — best with focaccia and tomatoes
•
Bombette — grilled pork rolls stuffed with cheese
•
Crudo di mare — raw seafood. Sea urchin season is usually Oct–Apr, so May is borderline.
•
Sgagliozze — fried polenta squares, Bari Vecchia street food

🏠 Bringing wine home

⚠️ Liquids over 100 ml can only go in checked baggage. In carry-on, they’ll be confiscated.

1.
Buy from an enoteca with EU shipping (Vinarius may offer it — ⚠️ verify)
2.
Buy at Bari Airport after security — limited selection, but perfectly legal
3.
Use a padded wine sleeve and check a small bag at the airport (€30–50 one way)
Great 4-bottle set: 1× Primitivo di Manduria + 1× Susumaniello + 1× Moscato di Trani + 1× Castel del Monte.

🚫 Do not pack

  • Hiking boots — overkill for cobblestones
  • Formal clothes — Puglia is casual even at nicer places
  • Full-size toiletries — airport security will eat them alive
  • Hairdryer — your accommodation should have one
  • Paper guidebooks — your phone is enough
  • Wine bottles in carry-on — guaranteed confiscation

🚫 Do not waste time on this

Alberobello

1.5 h+ by FSE with a transfer, then a trulli Disneyland full of tour buses. Save it for a deeper Puglia trip.

Lecce / Gallipoli / Salento

1.5–3 hours away. Not realistic for a relaxed public-transport day trip.

Matera as a rushed checklist day

If doing Matera (see Thu alternative), do it for mood: Sassi walk, one cave/rock-church stop, sunset viewpoint, wine, early dinner. Do not stack 8 attractions.

Murgia Timone without planning

The opposite-canyon viewpoint is stunning but sits across the ravine. Do it only with a planned transfer/tour, or skip it and use city viewpoints (Piazzetta Pascoli, Belvedere Guerricchio).

Grotta Palazzese restaurant

€€€€, mixed reviews, you’re paying for the cave. Admire it from above and move on.

Castellana Grotte

Too much transit, fixed tour times, and it eats half a day.

Tourist-menu places near the castle

Generic Lungomare-adjacent food. Walk 200 meters deeper into the old town instead.

The nonna pasta show as lunch

Watch it, yes. Eat beside plastic tables there, no.

Thinking May means beach weather

The Adriatic will likely be 18–20°C. Quick brave dip? Sure. Full beach day? Not really.

📝 Practical info & budget

📋 Travel documents

•
EU/Schengen citizens: valid ID card or passport. Children need their own document.
⚠️
mObywatel is NOT accepted at the Italian border — bring a physical document (Polish MFA warning).
•
EKUZ / EHIC — European Health Insurance Card. Bring it.
•
EES (Entry/Exit System): fully operational since 10 April 2026. Expect biometric registration at the border.
•
ETIAS: not yet active. Expected Q4 2026.
•
Emergency: 112 (free, EU-wide).
•
Medication: if carrying controlled substances, bring a certificate/prescription in Italian or English.

🎫 E-ticket & validation tip

•
Digital Regional Ticket (Trenitalia): auto-validates at the scheduled departure time — no manual stamping needed.
•
Paper regional ticket: stamp it in the yellow/green machine on the platform before boarding. Skipping this = fine.
•
Buy directly from operators (Trenitalia, Ferrotramviaria, FAL, FSE) — avoid resellers.
•
Check QR / ticket status in the app or email before boarding.

🛟 Plan B — when things go sideways

🌧️
Bad weather: swap Polignano for Trani or extend a Bari day — both are less sun/sea-dependent.
🚧
Track work or strike: check Trenitalia + Ferrotramviaria alerts in the morning. Replacement buses are typical on the Foggia–Bari–Lecce axis.
💼
Trip turns into remote work: cut to Bari + max one day trip (Polignano or Monopoli).
📸
Want more checklist sightseeing: activate Matera (Thu alternative) — but verify FAL the morning of travel.
